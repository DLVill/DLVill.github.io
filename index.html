I am a postdoctoral researcher at EPFL, in the Chair of Mathematical Analysis, Calculus of Variations and PDEs. I work on deterministic and stochastic partial differential equations arising in fluid mechanics, in particular dynamo theory, hydrodynamic and magnetohydrodynamic instabilities, mixing, and enhanced dissipation.

I completed my PhD in Pure Mathematics at Imperial College London in 2026, supervised by Michele Coti Zelati and Martin Hairer, as part of the Centre for Doctoral Training in Mathematics of Random Systems. Before that, I studied mathematics at the University of Cambridge and University College London.
